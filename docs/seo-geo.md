# SEO & GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) Strategy

## Target Keywords & Intent
- Primary Keywords: `Numen Network`, `Proof-of-Scan`, `PoScan`, `EVM Layer 1`, `Proof-of-Work BFT`, `Spectral3D`, `3D Blockchain Geometry`.
- Search Intent: Technical crypto researchers, blockchain developers, node operators, and miners looking for verifiable Layer 1 protocol specifications.

## Meta Tags & Social Metadata
- Unique `<title>` and `<meta name="description">` on every page.
- Canonical URLs targeting `https://numen-network.org/`.
- Open Graph (`og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`, `og:url`) and Twitter Card tags (`summary_large_image`, `@numen_network`).

## Structured Data (JSON-LD)
- `Organization` Schema for Numen Network.
- `SoftwareApplication` / `TechArticle` Schema describing the Proof-of-Scan protocol and node software.

## GEO / AI Search Optimization
- Clear, highly authoritative definitions of Proof-of-Scan, GRANDPA finality, and ASERT difficulty adjustment, structured for AI citation and summaries.
